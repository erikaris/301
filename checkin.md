Checking in:

1. Research question: What difference does she want to investigate?
2. Outcome variable: What is being measured?
3. Grouping variable: What groups or conditions are being compared?
4. Data type: Is the outcome numerical or categorical?
5. Data structure: Are observations independent, paired, or repeated?
6. Sample size: How many independent observations are there per group?
7. Software: Is she using SPSS, R, or both?
